Seeking a role at the intersection of translational health and data sciences to improve human ability. Across a variety of clinical biomechanics experiences - university and hospital settings, studying healthy and clinical populations, performing basic science and translational research - I have learned that better research tools yield better research outcomes. My interest in the area of data-driven rehabilitation began during my PhD while studying balance control during healthy gait, and blossomed at Shirley Ryan AbilityLab as I saw first-hand its potential to directly improve patient outcomes.
